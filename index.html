Tank Patrol

Guard the PC at the bottom of the map. Twenty tanks come for it each stage: brick breaks under fire, steel does not, and no tank can cross water.

Arrow keys or WASD drive, Space fires and P pauses; here the arrows drive and Alt with a red letter opens the menus. On a phone, use the pad and the Fire button.

[■]

Long Way Down

Keep going down. The shaft never stops rising, spikes wait at the top and there is nothing below the bottom ledge. Stone heals you, spikes do not, and dropping past several floors at once scores more. Deeper down come crumbling sandstone, mine carts, conveyors, ice, springs, falling rocks and tremors. Once a zone a cave gate opens in the wall: run in, jump the pits and critters, outrun the worm and drop out ten floors down.

Left and right arrows or A and D run, Space starts and P pauses; in a cave, up, W or Space jumps. Here the arrows run and Alt with a red letter opens the menus. On a phone, hold a button or either side of the shaft.

The caves' rock, timber and backdrops are from Pixel Fantasy Caves by Szadi art, recoloured for each zone.

Help

Everything works with the mouse, and with these keys:

F1This help
F10Go to the menu bar
Alt and a red letterDrop that menu down (Option on a Mac)
← →Move along the menu bar, or along a row of buttons (in the games, they drive and run)
↑ ↓Move through the open menu, or a list in a window
TabMove from one part of a window to the next
EnterOpen what is picked: a menu’s name opens its window, which takes the keyboard
EscClose the menu, then the window
F8Sound effects on or off
F9Music on or off

On a Mac laptop, hold fn for F1, F8, F9 and F10.

Leave the desk alone for two minutes and see what happens. And, as in any old game, there is a cheat code.
